### Explanation
The purpose of using two different speeds is to separate the robot’s linear movement from its turning movement. In this program, `forwardSpeed` controls the speed when the robot moves forward or backward, while `rotationSpeed` controls the speed when the robot turns left or right. This is useful because a real robot may need to move forward at one speed but rotate at another speed. For example, the robot can move forward slowly for better control, while still turning faster when changing direction. By using two independent speed variables, the robot becomes easier to control and behaves more smoothly.

The buttons are used to adjust these two speeds separately. The UP and DOWN buttons increase or decrease `forwardSpeed` by 5%, while the RIGHT and LEFT buttons increase or decrease `rotationSpeed` by 5%. Both speed values start at 50% and are limited between 0% and 100% to prevent invalid speed values. The joystick is used only to decide the direction of movement, such as forward, backward, left turn, right turn, or stop.
